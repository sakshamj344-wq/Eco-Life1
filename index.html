About Environmental Awareness

Environmental awareness teaches us to protect nature, reduce pollution, save water, and conserve energy. Small actions can make a big difference.

Eco-Friendly Tips

🌳 Plant Trees

Plant trees to improve air quality and protect wildlife.

♻️ Recycle Waste

Reuse materials and separate waste for recycling.

💧 Save Water

Turn off taps when not in use and avoid wasting water.

⚡ Save Energy

Switch off lights and appliances when not needed.
